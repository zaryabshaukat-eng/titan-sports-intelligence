# 015 — Risk Engine

Risk is an append-only assessment layer over immutable Consensus artifacts. It measures uncertainty, stability, calibration, agreement, and completeness/data-quality risk independently of all publication or betting policy.

Each output retains all component scores and the overall transparent aggregate. Lineage connects the Risk run to Consensus, constituent Probability runs, Research dataset/version, parameters, and seed. Invalid consensus evidence becomes a validation-failed Risk artifact with no output. Risk does not read raw providers, live odds, or market prices.
